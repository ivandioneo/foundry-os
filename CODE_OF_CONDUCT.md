# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our standards
Examples of positive behavior: empathy, respect for differing viewpoints,
graceful acceptance of constructive feedback, focus on what is best for the
project. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks,
publishing others' private information, and other conduct reasonably considered
inappropriate.

## Enforcement
Report concerns to `conduct@REPLACE_ME`. Maintainers are responsible for
clarifying standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate
this Code, and may ban contributors for behavior deemed harmful.
