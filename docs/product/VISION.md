# Vision

Drawn entirely from already-approved canonical sources —
[`docs/manifesto/MANIFESTO.md`](../manifesto/MANIFESTO.md) (MANIFESTO-001) and
`CLAUDE.md` §3–§4. This document organizes existing, approved vision statements
for product design use; it does not add new vision content
(`CLAUDE.md` §14 — reference, don't duplicate).

## Purpose

> Foundry exists to multiply the capability of founders.
>
> We believe extraordinary companies should not require extraordinary
> resources. A determined founder, equipped with the right systems,
> automation, and artificial intelligence, should be capable of building
> businesses that previously required significant capital and teams of
> specialists.
>
> Our mission is to make that possible.

— [Our Purpose](../manifesto/MANIFESTO.md#our-purpose), MANIFESTO-001

## Why Foundry Exists

> Building a software business has become unnecessarily difficult. Founders
> spend months assembling tools, learning technologies, configuring
> infrastructure, coordinating specialists, and solving problems unrelated to
> the value they want to create. The cost of building a company is often
> determined more by access to resources than by the quality of the idea.
>
> Foundry exists to remove unnecessary complexity so founders can focus on
> solving meaningful problems and creating value.

— [Why We Exist](../manifesto/MANIFESTO.md#why-we-exist), MANIFESTO-001

## Mission

> Enable founders to repeatedly create, launch, operate, and grow real
> software businesses through engineering systems, automation, and AI.
>
> Not prototypes. Not demos. Real businesses.

— [Our Mission](../manifesto/MANIFESTO.md#our-mission), MANIFESTO-001

`CLAUDE.md` §3 states the same mission in institutional-memory terms: _"Foundry
is the institutional memory of a venture studio: a system for discovering,
validating, building, and managing software companies. It turns every research
cycle, customer interview, and rejected idea into permanent, searchable,
interlinked knowledge so decision quality compounds over time."_

## Vision

> Create a future where one founder can build what previously required a
> well-funded company.
>
> We envision a world where entrepreneurship is limited only by imagination,
> determination, and execution—not by access to capital, large teams, or
> specialized expertise.

— [Our Vision](../manifesto/MANIFESTO.md#our-vision), MANIFESTO-001

`CLAUDE.md` §4 (Product Vision) frames the same vision as a path: _"Foundry
moves a founder along: Idea → Evidence → Validation → Decision → Execution →
Learning → Portfolio. It does not generate startup ideas — it generates better
decisions. The knowledge graph ... is the product. AI accelerates thinking; the
database is the asset."_

## Definition of Success

> Foundry succeeds when founders can repeatedly transform validated ideas into
> operating software businesses with dramatically less time, capital, and
> complexity than was previously possible.
>
> Our success is measured by the businesses our users build—not by the number
> of features we ship.

— [Definition of Success](../manifesto/MANIFESTO.md#definition-of-success), MANIFESTO-001

## The Foundry Promise

> You should never have to build alone what technology can build with you.
>
> Foundry exists so one determined founder can achieve what once required an
> entire company.

— [The Foundry Promise](../manifesto/MANIFESTO.md#the-foundry-promise), MANIFESTO-001

## What Stays Constant

> Technology will change. AI will evolve. Frameworks will come and go. Markets
> will shift. Our mission will remain constant: **Multiply the capability of
> founders.** Everything else is implementation.

— [Closing Statement](../manifesto/MANIFESTO.md#closing-statement), MANIFESTO-001

This is the standard every architecture document and PDR in this framework is
ultimately in service of.
